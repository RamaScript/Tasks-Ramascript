TASKS RAMASCRIPT
PRIVACY POLICY

TASKS RAMASCRIPT is a web application that lets you view and manage your Google Tasks. This page explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. What we access

  • Your Google Tasks lists and tasks (via the Google Tasks API). This is the sole purpose of the app.
  • Your basic profile info (name, email address, and profile picture) only to display "who is signed in".

2. How data is stored

When you sign in, the app receives a Google OAuth access token. That token is stored only in your browser (localStorage) and is used to call the Google Tasks API on your behalf. The app has no backend database or server — no task data is sent anywhere other than Google's servers.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not access tasks from other apps or sources.
  • We do not retain data when you sign out.

4. Your control

You can sign out at any time to remove the token from your browser. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Deleting a task in the app deletes it in Google Tasks.

5. Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact the developer at ramanand.in7@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
